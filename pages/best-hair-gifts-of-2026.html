The everyday upgrade

Bur Bur Mermaid Brush

Check Availability

Best for: Anyone still using a drugstore brush, especially with medium to thick hair.

What’s with the boar bristle hype? Since it uses boar’s hair, the bristles have a structure similar to human hair, and they pick up your scalp’s natural oil. When you brush, it evenly distributes the oil from your roots down to your ends; you can think of it as built-in conditioner.

The bristles also smooth and align the hair cuticle, which tames frizz and flyaways and leaves a glossy finish; and the nylon pins help work through knots without snagging.

It’s the kind of everyday tool most people wouldn’t think to upgrade. Each one is handcrafted in Korea with a polished beech wood handle, so it’s beautiful enough to leave out on the vanity.

The shower glowup

MDhair Filtered Showerhead

Check Availability

Best for: Anyone with a shower that leaves their hair feeling dry and dull.

Unfiltered tap water can weaken your hair, irritate your scalp, and dull your hair color — not exactly what you signed up for.

That’s why a group of dermatologists developed this sleek, stylish showerhead that runs water through a 20-stage filter before it reaches your hair.

It’s built to remove contaminants like chlorine, microplastics, and heavy metals from your tap water, so hair feels softer, shinier, and less prone to dryness. Anyone living with hard or heavily chlorinated water will notice it most.

It installs in minutes with no plumber needed, and many users have seen improved water pressure after installing. It’s the kind of upgrade most people don’t think to buy for themselves, which is exactly what makes it a great gift.
