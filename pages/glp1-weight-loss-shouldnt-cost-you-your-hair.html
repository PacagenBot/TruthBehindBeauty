GLP-1 Weight Loss Shouldn't Cost You Your Hair.

From a $24 staple to a biotech newcomer with a 190-person clinical trial — these are the 5 hair serums GLP-1 users should know about during their weight loss journey.

By Charlotte William
Share:
Updated September 2, 2026

If you're on a GLP-1 and noticing more hair in the drain, you're not imagining it — and you're not alone. Rapid weight loss can trigger telogen effluvium, a temporary shedding phase where follicles shift into rest mode. The good news: it's usually reversible, and supporting your scalp while your body recalibrates can make a real difference. We looked at everything from drugstore staples to clinical newcomers — here are 5 hair serums you should be using in 2026.
