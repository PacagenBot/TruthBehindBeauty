A thinner ponytail. A wider-looking part. More hair in your brush than usual. Once you notice changes in your hair, it’s easy to fall down the hair-serum rabbit hole. Every bottle promises fuller-looking hair—but which ones are worth your time and money?

We compared six of the best hair serums to know in 2026, from a $24 staple to a biotech newcomer. We’re breaking down the ingredients, scalp feel, daily routine, and price—with honest pros and cons to help you choose.

Best Minoxidil

Women’s ROGAINE 2% Minoxidil Solution

Pros
  • Backed by years of clinical evidence
  • FDA-approved ingredient
Cons
  • Initial “dread shed” is common
  • Toxic to pets, even in low doses
  • Users note instances of scalp irritation and discomfort
  • Unwanted facial hair growth associated with minoxidil use

ROGAINE is the medicated option in our top five. It’s an unscented liquid with 2% minoxidil, applied directly to the scalp with a dropper twice a day. The application itself is simple, but you’ll want to give it time to dry before styling. One small drawback is the noticeable alcohol smell during application, which may bother those sensitive to strong scents. Some people experience temporary shedding when they start, and scalp irritation and unwanted facial hair are possible side effects.

Why it’s our Editor’s Top Pick: It’s an FDA-approved over-the-counter treatment with clinical evidence for hair regrowth in women with gradual thinning on top of the scalp. At about $17 a month, it’s one of the more affordable options in our roundup.

KilgourMD Prevention and Treatment Scalp Serums

Buy Now
Pros
  • Founded by a Stanford-trained dermatologist
  • 180-day clinical trial
  • Many users start noticing improvement after 6 weeks
Cons
  • Two-bottle routine
  • Greasy texture
  • Disappointing scent
  • Some users cite hair discoloration when using the product
  • Premium price point

Why it’s our Editor’s Top Pick: KilgourMD recently updated its packaging and reformulated both serums. Prevention now features GlycoRoot™, a basil root extract in a glycogen-based delivery system, while Treatment pairs saw palmetto with a delivery peptide. It’s our pick for a two-step routine because the formulas are designed to work together, and you can apply them back-to-back or split them between morning and night. The brand’s four-month study tested the earlier formulas together.

A few things to consider. At $178 for the pair, it’s a pricey routine with two serums to apply every day. When I tried the original duo, it felt a little heavy at my roots. Some Reddit users also reported a strong scent or orange discoloration with earlier versions. It’s still unclear whether the new formulas address those complaints.

OneSkin Hair Scalp Serum

Buy Now
Pros
  • Longevity-based science
  • Formulation includes a key peptide targeting senescence in hair follicles
  • Dermaroller included with multi-month plans
Cons
  • Recommended use, twice a day
  • Small study group
  • Premium price point
  • Only available online

Why it’s our Editor’s Top Pick: OneSkin brought the OS-01 peptide from its skincare line into a leave-in scalp serum for fuller-looking hair. What earned it a spot here is the finish. The formula is fragrance-free, and the brand says it dries without leaving residue. If you’re picky about scent or how products feel at your roots, this is the one I’d look at. Massage it directly into your scalp once or twice a day.

What to keep in mind. The six-month study involved 32 people who applied the serum twice a day and used a dermaroller daily. That’s a more involved routine than a quick morning application. If you plan to use the serum on its own, the study gives you limited insight into what results to expect.

So, which hair serum is worth trying?

RE:YOU is our 2026 Editor’s Pick for its lightweight, once-daily formula and promising early results from a head-to-head study. If you’re just dipping a toe into scalp serums, The Ordinary is where I’d start on a budget. OneSkin is our fragrance-free pick, Vegamour fits easily around wash days, and KilgourMD makes sense if you like a dedicated two-step routine. If you’re looking for an over-the-counter regrowth treatment for gradual thinning, Women’s ROGAINE 2% is our minoxidil pick.

My advice? Picture yourself using it on a busy weekday. If you know you’ll forget a second application or hate how a product feels at your roots, pick something simpler. Go with a routine you can stick with and a price you’re comfortable paying beyond the first bottle.
