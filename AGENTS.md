# Article publishing and featuring

Automatic featuring is paused at the user’s request.

- Creating or updating an article must not automatically add it to, reorder it within, or otherwise change `index.html` or the article listing in `pages/reviews.html`.
- Homepage Featured Articles, Trending Now, More Articles, and the Reviews listing are manually curated. Change those selections only when the user explicitly requests a homepage or Reviews listing change.
- Keep reviews currently marked `hidden` hidden unless the user requests otherwise.
- Continue normal article creation and updates, including applicable sitemap entries. Publishing an article and featuring it are separate actions.
- Keep this policy in place until the user explicitly resumes automatic featuring.
